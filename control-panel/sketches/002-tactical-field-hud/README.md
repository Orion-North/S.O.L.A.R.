## Variant: Tactical Field HUD // Action-First Operator Deck

### Design Stance
A ruggedized handheld field terminal designed for direct real-time teleoperation in outdoor or dynamic environments. Prioritizes thumb-driven motor control, high daylight contrast, immediate tactile feedback, and situational awareness over dense numerical logs.

### Key Choices
- **Layout**: Immersive full-bleed camera feed with HUD overlays, flanked by dual ergonomic thumb clusters (Left: vector drive joystick & speed control; Right: tactical action macro bank & oversized hazard E-Stop slap button).
- **Color**: Heavy industrial graphite (`#0d0f12`), hazard warning yellow (`#eab308`), electric cyan (`#06b6d4`), and emergency crimson (`#ef4444`).
- **Typography**: Industrial headline styling paired with clean monospace telemetry overlays.
- **Interaction**: Directional analog joystick/WASD vector response, instant stance macros (STAND, TURTLE, SIT, RECOVER/SELF-RIGHT), and single-strike E-Stop slap.

### Trade-offs
- **Strong at**: Rapid teleoperation, piloting, outdoor field trials, fast reaction time.
- **Weak at**: Reduced space for simultaneous 12-motor angle calibration or long history log analysis.

### Best For
Field trials, live demonstrations, obstacle navigation, and RC-style manual piloting.
